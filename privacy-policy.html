Privacy Policy — Near Me

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Near Me ("we", "our") is a WhatsApp-based marketplace service that helps people buy and sell items in their local area. This policy explains what information we collect when you interact with our WhatsApp service, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • WhatsApp phone number and profile name — provided by WhatsApp when you message us.
  • Messages you send us — text, photos, voice notes, and button selections, used to operate the marketplace (creating listings, searching, connecting buyers and sellers).
  • Location — only when you explicitly share it, used to show you nearby listings and to place your listings on the map for nearby buyers. We never track your location in the background.
  • Listing content — item details, prices, and photos you submit for sale.

How we use your information

  • To operate the marketplace: publish your listings, match buy requests with nearby sellers, and notify you of relevant matches.
  • To process your messages, including AI-assisted understanding of your requests and transcription of voice notes.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, and fraudulent activity.

Contact sharing

Your phone number is shared with another user only with your explicit consent — when a buyer expresses interest in your listing and you tap "Accept". We never sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Data storage and retention

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Listings expire automatically after a period of inactivity. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by messaging us "delete my account" on WhatsApp.

Third-party services

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) to send and receive messages, cloud infrastructure to store data and media, and AI providers to process message content. These providers process data solely on our behalf.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it by contacting us through WhatsApp or at the email below.
